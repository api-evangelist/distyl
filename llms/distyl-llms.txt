# Distyl

> Distyl builds the intelligence layer for enterprises — systems that encode institutional expertise, execute mission-critical work, and compound in value with every decision.

Distyl is an applied technology company architecting the AI-native enterprise. Rather than layering automation onto broken processes, Distyl reimagines enterprise operations from the ground up with AI built at the core. The company partners with the most ambitious Fortune 500 organizations — across Telecom, Healthcare, Manufacturing, Insurance, Retail, and Financial Services — to design and operationalize their AI transformations, deploying production-grade agentic AI systems in weeks, not years.

Distyl was founded by operators and researchers from Palantir, CIA, OpenAI, Google DeepMind, Tesla, NASA, Stanford, and MIT. It is backed by Coatue, OpenAI, Lightspeed, Microsoft, and Khosla Ventures.

Key outcomes delivered: powered 140M+ consumer interactions in one of the largest agentic AI deployments to date; 95% of AI systems moved from strategy into production (vs. the industry norm where 95% of enterprise AI stalls at the pilot stage); EBITDA impact in 6 months.
